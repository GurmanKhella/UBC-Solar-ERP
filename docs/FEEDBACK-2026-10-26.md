# Walkthrough feedback, 2026-10-26

Attendees: Captain, Electrical Exec, and three team members. These are the points
that should shape the build.

1. **Build a prototype, not more plan.** "Feedback to a plan is just another
   plan." People want something to click through before DR1.
2. **Narrow the scope.** Start with the problems people actually have, not every
   problem that could exist. Fail fast on a small slice, then add features.
3. **The real users are members and senior designers, not leads.** Leads already
   know where things are. New members don't know where anything is (e.g. can't
   find a multimeter, the integration-test harness box moves every time).
4. **Low friction or it dies.** The old electrical-inventory system worked for a
   week, then people stopped following it. Needs:
   - an urgent path ("order this now, we leave for comp in an hour") that
     doesn't require filling in every field;
   - no QR labelling of every piece of metal stock;
   - a way to stay trustworthy when someone puts something in the wrong place.
5. **Show the delta from a spreadsheet.** If it looks like a spreadsheet with
   extra steps, it won't win anyone over.
6. **Make subsystems easy to change** (add/rename/remove without code).
7. **Finding everything about one board** (Altium 365, DR doc, integration-test
   results, Monday) is a real pain point. The project record linking to all of
   them addresses it.
8. **Hosting:** prototype locally; the real system online (cloud, not a local
   server).
9. **Existing CRM** already syncs trackers every 30 minutes and batches Google API
   calls to avoid rate limits. Talk to the Captain before replacing it.
10. **Mech and electrical in one system** is a concern for complexity, but the
    team agrees they shouldn't run separately.
